citylearn.internal.provenance module

Passive electricity provenance, definition 1.0.

An interval has a perfectly mixed AC bus per member. Storage is proportionally mixed and its operations remain ordered. An affine balance resolves same-step storage cycles without inventing solar energy or iterating physical actions. Only aggregate source/destination counters and active storage stocks are kept.
