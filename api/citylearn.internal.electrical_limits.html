citylearn.internal.electrical_limits module

Side-effect-free allocation of signed electrical-service requests, in kW.

Positive power imports/consumes; negative power exports/supplies. Callers provide their own base-load estimate and phase split. This calculation reduces requests proportionally; it neither dispatches devices nor guarantees that fixed loads alone satisfy the service limits. Device physics and sequential outage supply checks remain the runtime’s responsibility.

citylearn.internal.electrical_limits.allocate_electrical_service(base_total_kw: float, base_phase_kw: Mapping[str, float], controls: Mapping[str, PowerRequest], total_limits: Mapping[str, float | None], per_phase_limits: Mapping[str, Mapping[str, float | None]], phase_names: Sequence[str]) → ElectricalServiceAllocation[source]

Return reductions and pressure without mutating any input or device.

Preserve the established eight-pass maximum, scope order (total, then the supplied phases) and import-before-export order. Every scope reads totals after earlier reductions; opposite-sign controls can couple scopes, requiring a later pass. Scales only decrease, preserving proportional sharing within each constrained group. There is no redistribution or service prioritisation.

Sum requested/residual excess over total and phase limits, in kW.

This pressure metric counts each scope separately; it is not unmet energy. Missing/non-finite limits are unbounded, as in the service configuration.
