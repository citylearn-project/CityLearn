    KPI catalogue

    This page lists every KPI name that evaluate_v2() emits, grouped by family, with a short definition. It is generated from citylearn/misc/kpi_catalogue.yml by scripts/docs/kpi_catalogue.py, and a test checks that every name the simulator emits is listed here. The equations, the district aggregation and the undefined-value rules of each family are in the KPI reference, which each family links to.

    Names read <level>_<family>_<subfamily>_<metric>[_<variant>]_<unit>. The level is building or district; MultiCommunityEnv adds portfolio rows built from the district rows (see Levels). In the tables, {a,b} stands for each alternative and {,_x} for an optional part; the full names follow each table. Which rows appear depends on the scenario’s assets and services.

    Rows marked Legacy keep an older definition so earlier results stay comparable, and rows marked Deprecated return the same value as another row; both name the canonical row to report instead. evaluate_v2() gives each row its status (current, canonical, legacy or deprecated) and replacement, and export manifests record them in kpi_definitions.

    Energy provenance

    Traces every kWh of electricity from its origin (own or neighbour PV, grid import, or storage inventory of unknown origin) to its destination (load, storage, mobility, export, losses and others), with the grid carbon it carries; rows appear whenever the environment keeps the provenance ledger (energy_provenance=True, the default).

    Equations and rules: Energy provenance.

    building, district

    kWh

    PV energy produced by the member, including the part later curtailed; _business_as_usual and _delta_to_business_as_usual as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_{pv,grid,unknown}_{load,mobility,grid_export,loss,asset_exit,external_reset,curtailment,stored}{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kwh

    building, district

    kWh

    Energy of origin PV, grid (including grid import of unknown carbon) or unknown that reached the named destination in this member, counted by the receiving member; stored is the inventory still in the member’s storage at evaluation, and only PV can be curtailed, so the grid and unknown curtailment rows are always 0. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    building, district

    kWh

    Split of PV reaching the member’s load: direct did not pass through storage in that interval, and after_storage is max(pv_load - pv_load_direct, 0). BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_unknown_input{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kwh

    building, district

    kWh

    Energy that entered the ledger with unknown origin: the opening inventory of each storage when the ledger first sees it (including the full stored energy of every EV at connection) and every externally imposed state of charge. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    building, district

    kWh

    Energy received from (local_import) or sent to (local_export) other members in the same interval, in total and by origin; these are internal transfers, already counted in the receiving member’s destinations. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_external_import{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kwh

    building, district

    kWh

    Grid import of the member after local sharing, max(net, 0) - local_import summed over intervals, including intervals of unknown carbon intensity. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_external_purchase_emissions{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kgco2

    building, district

    kgCO2

    Emissions of the member’s grid import after local sharing, at the member’s carbon intensity; intervals with a non-finite intensity add nothing, and exports earn no credit. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_external_purchase_unknown_carbon{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kwh

    building, district

    kWh

    Part of external_import imported in intervals whose carbon intensity is not finite, and therefore missing from the emissions. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_carbon_{load,mobility,grid_export,loss,asset_exit,external_reset,curtailment,stored}{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kgco2

    building, district

    kgCO2

    Grid carbon carried by the energy that reached the named destination in this member; only grid energy carries carbon, so carbon_curtailment is always 0. BAU variants as in the shared rules.

    energy_provenance_carbon_unknown_{load,mobility,grid_export,loss,asset_exit,external_reset,curtailment,stored}{,_business_as_usual,_delta_to_business_as_usual}_kwh

    building, district

    kWh

    district

    kWh

    district

    kgCO2
