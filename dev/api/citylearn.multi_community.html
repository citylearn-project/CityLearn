    class citylearn.multi_community.MultiCommunityEnv(*, communities: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]], render_directory: str | Path = None, render_session_name: str = None)[source]

    Bases: Env

    Synchronize multiple independent CityLearnEnv communities.

    V1 is an orchestration layer only: every child environment keeps its own physics, demand response state, reward and KPIs. The wrapper coordinates reset/step calls and adds portfolio-level KPI rows from district totals.

    evaluate_v2(control_condition: EvaluationCondition = None, baseline_condition: EvaluationCondition = None, comfort_band: float = None, include_business_as_usual: bool = True) → DataFrame[source]

    Return each community’s KPIs plus portfolio rows aggregated from their district KPIs.

    The table has the columns of citylearn.citylearn.CityLearnEnv.evaluate_v2() and a community_id column. Each community contributes its 'building' and 'district' rows under its own community_id. The 'portfolio' rows (community_id '__portfolio__', names starting portfolio_) aggregate the district rows: ratios of totals are recomputed from the summed totals, rates and stationary-battery equivalent full cycles are pooled by their denominators, other _kwh/_eur/_kgco2/_count rows are summed, other ratios and percentages are means weighted by the community weight, and other rows (_kw, _c, _hours) have no portfolio row. When some communities have a community market and others do not, a community without a market enters the portfolio community-market rows with its plain grid settlement: no local trade, its members’ grid import and export, and its control cost as settled and counterfactual cost. Its own district rows are unchanged. The KPI reference guide gives the rule of each family.
