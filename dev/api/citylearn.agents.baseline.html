    citylearn.agents.baseline module

    Non-learning reference controllers: business-as-usual (BAU) and rule-based baselines.

    All controllers in this module read the same named, unnormalized observations, built from the entity observation payload (see citylearn.agents._entity_observations). They work with the flat and the entity interface and return flat-like action vectors in env.action_names order.

    Agent

    Behaviour

    BusinessAsUsualAgent

    Reference BAU. Charges a connected EV at once, at full power, to 100% (default) or up to the user’s requested departure SOC (ev_charge_target='requested'); starts appliances at the first admissible step; the home battery stores PV surplus and serves local import. With hvac='dataset_demand' (default under baseline_version=2) the HVAC delivers the dataset’s heating and cooling demand.

    RBCBasicPolicy

    Current price and service urgency for EVs and appliances; battery self-consumption.

    RBCSmartPolicy

    Price and local PV-surplus forecasts schedule EVs and appliances; battery combines self-consumption, arbitrage and reserve. Uses only building-local and public observations.

    RBCCommunityPolicy

    Smart plus energy shareable between members. When the community market is enabled, batteries support expensive periods once community import reaches a trigger (15 kW by default). Without a market it shares no energy.

    The three RBCs also run the HVAC (heating_device, cooling_device, cooling_or_heating_device) at the dataset’s heating and cooling demand, as the BAU does under the default baseline_version=2 (hvac='dataset_demand', the default; pass hvac='off' for the zero HVAC of earlier releases). They do not optimise the HVAC. On datasets without HVAC actions this changes nothing.

    The policies are ported from the Soft-CPS Algorithms baselines and keep their audited defaults. Keyword arguments override the engine hyperparameters, for example RBCCommunityPolicy(env, community_import_threshold_kw=10.0).

    class citylearn.agents.baseline.BusinessAsUsualAgent(env: CityLearnEnv, ev_charge_target: str = 'full', hvac: str | None = None, **hyperparameters: Any)[source]

    Bases: _RuleBaselineAgent

    Reference BAU: immediate EV charging, earliest appliance start, PV self-consumption.

    A connected EV is charged at once and at maximum power. With ev_charge_target='full' (default, immediate-full-charge-v4) it charges to 100%, as the BAU of CityLearn 3.1–3.3 did. With ev_charge_target='requested' (immediate-user-target-v3) it stops at the user’s requested departure SOC: the last command is limited to the remaining energy gap, and when that command is below the charger minimum the EV stops slightly below the target instead of overshooting it. Appliances start at the first admissible step and the home battery uses observed current-step demand and PV when estimating local surplus or import.

    The HVAC follows hvac. With 'off' the heating_device, cooling_device and cooling_or_heating_device actions are zero, as in CityLearn 3.6.0 and under baseline_version=1, so on datasets with HVAC actions the BAU buildings are neither heated nor cooled (#362). With 'dataset_demand' each step the HVAC action is the fraction of the device’s nominal power that delivers the dataset’s own heating or cooling demand (heating_demand_without_control and cooling_demand_without_control of the energy simulation, the as-simulated demand of the building file), clipped to the action bounds; the device limits (nominal power, outage supply) cap it as for any action. That is a thermostat that meets the load without optimisation. The demand is used only in the steps whose hvac_mode allows that service. The default, None, is 'dataset_demand' when the environment’s baseline_version is 2 (the default) and it has HVAC actions, and 'off' under version 1 or without HVAC actions, which keeps the definition and identity of earlier releases there.

    Choose the BAU that evaluate_v2() uses with the environment’s business_as_usual_ev_charge_target and baseline_version.

    class citylearn.agents.baseline.RBCBasicPolicy(env: CityLearnEnv, hvac: str = 'dataset_demand', **hyperparameters: Any)[source]

    Bases: _RuleBasedPolicy

    Current-price and service-urgency scheduling with battery self-consumption.

    EVs receive the minimum charge that meets their departure target, raised to ev_price_charge_rate when the current price is cheap. Appliances start when urgent or cheap. The default price thresholds (basic_cheap_price_eur_kwh=0.0765, basic_expensive_price_eur_kwh=0.1078) are the terciles of the 2023 Portuguese day-ahead price; set them for other tariffs, or use basic_price_policy='forecast_relative' to compare the current price with the price forecast instead.

    class citylearn.agents.baseline.RBCCommunityPolicy(env: CityLearnEnv, hvac: str = 'dataset_demand', **hyperparameters: Any)[source]

    Bases: _RuleBasedPolicy

    Smart controller extended with community energy sharing.

    With the community market enabled, shareable surplus from other members is treated as discounted energy, and batteries help during expensive periods once community import reaches community_import_threshold_kw (15 kW by default; a trigger, not a hard cap). Without a community market it uses the same economic rules as RBCSmartPolicy, without sharing energy, but it is not restricted to building-local observations.

    Forecast-aware, strictly building-local rule-based controller.

    Price and local PV-surplus forecasts schedule EV charging and appliance starts; the home battery combines self-consumption, price arbitrage and a service reserve. Community observations are ignored.

    class citylearn.agents.baseline.ZeroActionAgent(env: CityLearnEnv, **kwargs: Any)[source]

    Bases: Agent

    Agent that requests zero for every action dimension.

    Up to CityLearn 3.6.0 this class was called ZeroActionBaselineAgent. That name is a deprecated alias that will be removed in a later release: in CityLearn, “baseline” names the KPI counterfactual, while this agent requests zero actions.

    Under the entity interface it returns flat-like zero vectors in env.action_names order, which the entity interface accepts.
