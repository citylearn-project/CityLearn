    Portugal REC Datasets

    Four synthetic datasets of Portuguese renewable energy communities (RECs), with 4, 15, 30 and 100 residential and commercial members. They cover 2023-2024 at 15-minute resolution. Building demand and PV generation are linked to explicit EV charging and appliance requests, weather, prices and carbon intensity. Scenarios add membership changes, restricted import limits, grid interruptions, equipment faults and missing observations, each labelled. Comparison scenarios remove individual conditions; each differs from its main scenario only in the condition it removes. Controllers can therefore be tested on whether they keep delivering the requested services, on identical and reproducible inputs.

    The datasets are edition v3.4 of Synthetic electricity demand and flexibility datasets for Portuguese energy communities (Fonseca et al.), distributed with CityLearn from 3.5.0. Their time series are Parquet files, so install CityLearn with the parquet extra, pip install "citylearn[parquet]", which adds pyarrow.

    The Safety scenario restricts import limits only; it does not model voltage, protection or phase-level network safety. The Robustness, Combined and All-in scenarios label their disturbances; withhold these labels from controllers in fault-detection studies.

    Loading

    Pass a dataset name for its Normal scenario. The first call downloads the dataset into the CityLearn cache:

    from citylearn.citylearn import CityLearnEnv

env = CityLearnEnv('pt_rec_30', episode_time_steps=96)

    Load another scenario from the cached dataset folder:

    from pathlib import Path
from citylearn.data import DataSet

root = Path(DataSet().get_dataset('pt_rec_30')).parent
env = CityLearnEnv(str(root/'evaluation'/'rec_30_combined.json'), episode_time_steps=96)

    All scenarios use the entity interface, and the community market is enabled. Begin with a short window: full two-year runs without the BAU reference took 6 to 127 minutes, from REC-4 to REC-100, on CityLearn 3.3.0. A window with n stored states supports n - 1 transitions.

    Conventions

    • Calendar: 70,176 interval-start timestamps from 1 January 2023 00:00 UTC to 31 December 2024 23:45 UTC. Lisbon civil days have 92 or 100 intervals at daylight-saving transitions.

    • Units: demand, PV and programme profiles in kWh per interval (multiply by four for average kW); battery capacity in kWh; power limits in kW; prices in EUR/kWh; carbon intensity in kg CO2e/kWh; SOC as a fraction. Missing observations use -9999.

    • Annual fields: annual summary fields in the catalogues refer to 2023; the series cover both years.

    • Charging sessions: a charging session runs from its arrival interval up to, but excluding, its departure interval. Sessions still connected at the end of the record are right-censored; the end of the file is not a deadline.

    • Advance information: by default each charger announces an arriving vehicle one hour (four intervals) ahead, with its expected arrival time and SOC; at connection, each session gives its departure time and target SOC.

    • Prices: grid purchase is max(Portuguese OMIE day-ahead price / 1000, 0) EUR/kWh, without network tariffs, taxes or retail margins. Local exchanges settle at 80 % of it and grid export is valued at zero.

    • Identifiers: member and equipment identifiers are unique within a dataset only.

    Reporting results

    Report energy, cost and carbon together with service delivery: lower consumption can come from a vehicle that left below its target or an appliance that never ran. Evaluate service per request, comparing delivered with requested EV energy and each appliance start with its window and deadline.

    Limitation

    Consequence

    Designed inventories; members shared between datasets

    Suitable for controlled comparisons; not representative of Portuguese RECs, and the datasets are not independent samples.

    Prescribed charging targets and appliance windows

    Service requirements are modelling assumptions, not measured user preferences.

    Residential demand 30-36 % above SHEERM measurements

    Self-sufficiency is likely lower than for the measured homes; normalise or test sensitivity where absolute levels matter.

    Longer EV connections than in measured sessions

    May favour scheduling-based controllers.

    Arrival SOC prescribed for each charging session

    Travel continuity over consecutive days is not tested.

    Experimental price rules; average carbon intensity

    Results are not savings under a real tariff or marginal emission reductions.

    Event rates derived from pilot communities

    Robustness studies should also vary them.
